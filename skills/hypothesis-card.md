---
title: Gipoteza kartasi
desc: Taxminni tekshiriladigan shaklga keltirish.
---
Har gipotezani shu shaklda yozing:
1. **Kuzatuv** (fakt, manba bilan)
2. **Taxmin** (nima uchun shunday deb o'ylaymiz)
3. **Bashorat** (agar to'g'ri bo'lsa, nimani ko'ramiz)
4. **Inkor sharti** (qanday natija gipotezani rad etadi)
5. **Tekshirish usuli** va kerakli ma'lumot.
Dalilsiz gipotezani "gipoteza" deb belgilang; faktdan ajrating.
