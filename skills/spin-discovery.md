---
title: Kashfiyot savollari (SPIN)
desc: Mijoz ehtiyojini ochish.
---
Savol turlari: **S**ituation (hozirgi holat) → **P**roblem (qiyinchilik) → **I**mplication (oqibati, narxi) → **N**eed-payoff (yechim foydasi).
Qo'shimcha: kim qaror qiladi, mezonlar, byudjet, muddat. Har bosqichga 3–4 savol yozing; savol emas, taklif bilan boshlamang.
