# Dcard — product marketing context

*Document version 1.0 · 2026-10-29 · drafted from dcard.uz. Items marked **[?]** are unconfirmed — ask the client before using them in public copy.*

## Product
- **What:** mobile app for discounts, 1+1 offers and coin bonuses at partner places (restaurants, supermarkets, beauty, electronics, services). Customer shows a personal QR code at checkout.
- **Apps:** iOS https://apps.apple.com/uz/app/dcard-%D0%B0%D0%B1%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%82-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D1%8D%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%8E/id6759165589 · Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xml.dcard · Telegram https://t.me/dcarduz
- **Features (site):** chegirmalar; 1+1 takliflar; shaxsiy QR-kod; coin bonuslari (daily tasks + coin-earning purchases; coins pay for the in-app subscription — "Coin bilan obuna"); partners on a map; saved offers.
- **Business side:** "Hamkor bo'lish" in the app menu — present the business and branches, publish offers, be found on the map.
- **Model:** subscription ("abonement", coins can pay for it) **[?] price, trial, what's free vs paid — ask.**

## Audience [?]
Young urban shoppers in Uzbekistan (Tashkent first?) who eat out, buy coffee, shop at supermarkets; deal-seekers; couples/friends (1+1). Partners: cafés, restaurants, salons, shops.

## Promise (site copy)
"Har bir xariddan ko'proq foyda." · "Xarid — odatdagidek. Foydasi boshqacha." · "Bitta xarid. Ikki karra quvonch." · "Kichik odatlar. Yig'ilib boradigan foyda." · 3 steps: Toping → Ko'rsating → Yig'ing. · "Yaxshi taklif uzoqda emas."

## Voice
Uzbek (Latin), playful, light, rhythmic two-line slogans; everyday moments (qahva, kechki ovqat, katta xarid). Friendly "siz".

## Brand
Blue mark with glyph pattern + yellow sparkles; Inter. Assets and the film (incl. the app's own close animation): `dcard-film/`.

## Rules for any marketing output
- **No invented results:** no %, "2x", saving amounts, partner counts, user counts unless the client gives them. The site itself avoids figures and says "Chegirma va bonuslar hamkorning amaldagi taklif shartlariga bog'liq" — keep that disclaimer on offer ads.
- Never name a partner brand or show its logo without written permission.
