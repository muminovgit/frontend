# Davo Delivery — product marketing context

*Document version 1.0 · 2026-10-29 · drafted from davodelivery.uz + the app screenshots the client sent. Items marked **[?]** are unconfirmed — ask the client before using them in public copy.*

## Product
- **What:** mobile app (online pharmacy marketplace) — search a medicine, compare prices across partner pharmacies (list or map), order, get it delivered home. Tashkent only (site FAQ: "Hozircha Toshkent shahrida").
- **Apps:** iOS https://apps.apple.com/us/app/davo-delivery/id6738921708 · Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uz.davo.mobile
- **Contacts:** +998 55 512 11 33 · Telegram @davodeliver · bot @davodelivery_bot · site davodelivery.uz (UZ/RU/EN)
- **Live features:** medicine search (composition, manufacturer, how many pharmacies stock it, alternatives, Rx drugs flagged); price comparison (total per pharmacy, hours, distance, price pins on map); order tracking Qabul qilindi → Tayyor → Yo'lda → Yetkazildi with ETA; payment by Uzcard / Humo; categories: asosiy dori vositalari, go'zallik va kosmetika, vitaminlar va badlar.
- **New:** **Dori eslatma** (pill reminder) — notification "Dori ichish vaqti", dose counter (e.g. 1/2 → 2/2).
- **Coming soon — never present as live:** Hamshira chaqirish, Tez yordam chaqirish, Chaqaloqlar uchun, Aksiya va takliflar (shown as "tez orada" in the app).
- **B2B side:** manufacturers, distributors and shops can sell in the app (BAD/vitamins, sports nutrition, cosmetics, kids' products) — new buyers, orders panel, courier pickup.

## Audience [?]
- Primary: Tashkent residents ordering medicine for themselves or family — parents of small children (sick child at night, syrups), people caring for elderly parents, people on regular medication (→ reminder).
- Secondary: price-conscious buyers who used to phone around pharmacies.

## Problem → promise
- Pain: "dorixonama-dorixona yurish yoki qo'ng'iroq qilish" — walking/calling pharmacy to pharmacy, not knowing price or stock.
- Promise (site copy): "Dori qidirish endi bir necha soniyada", "Narxlarni solishtiring", "Buyurtmangiz doim ko'z oldingizda", "Sog'lig'ingiz uchun eng oson yo'l".
- 4 steps: Qidiring → Ro'yxat tuzing → Dorixonani tanlang → Qabul qiling.

## Voice
Uzbek (Latin) first, Russian second. Warm, caring, simple; health topic → calm confidence, no fear-selling, no medical claims, no "cure" language. Short sentences. "Siz" form.

## Brand
Logo: four petals #ffce68 (yellow) #fb4669 (pink) #5e3a9a (violet) #01bdac (green) + white cross; accent teal #09bbd4. Fonts: Montserrat, Inter. Assets and a ready reel: `davo-reel/`.

## Rules for any marketing output
- No invented numbers (users, pharmacies, % savings, delivery minutes) — the site's "20-25 min" and prices are UI demo values, not claims. Ask for real figures.
- Medicine ads: no promise of treatment effect; Rx drugs are not promoted; follow Uzbekistan advertising law for medicines **[?]** — flag for the client's legal check.
- Delivery ETA, coverage, fees: confirm with client before publishing.
