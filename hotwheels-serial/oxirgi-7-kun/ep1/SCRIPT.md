# OXIRGI 7 KUN — 1-qism: "Xat" (ssenariy, tasdiqlash uchun)

## Faktlar (HAQIQAT kartasi uchun)
- 1968–1977 yillarda chiqarilgan Hot Wheels mashinalari shinasidagi qizil chiziq sabab kolleksionerlar orasida "Redline" deb ataladi.
  Manba: NCHWA Redline Central — https://nchwa.com/hwhistory.html ; Muncle Mike's — https://munclemikes.com/pages/hot-wheels-history-collector-lore
- 1968-yilgi "Strawberry over Chrome" rangidagi Custom Mustang Redline'larning eng qimmatlaridan biri, taxminan $40 000 baholanadi.
  Manba: SlashGear — https://www.slashgear.com/1310179/expensive-hot-wheels-cars/ ; Wargamer — https://www.wargamer.com/hot-wheels/most-expensive
- Hikoya to'qima, faktlar haqiqiy (videoda kichik yozuv).
